=== PART 1 — PROFESSOR VIEW (simple-exam-tab.tsx) ===

In the expanded submission view, add a new "VoxScore Breakdown" section. Place it BETWEEN the submission header row and the AI Feedback section — not inside AI Feedback, not below Per-Question Results.

--- 1A. Compact summary strip (always visible) ---

When voxScoreProfile is not null, show a compact strip:

VoxScore [totalScore]/100 · [band label] · Strongest: [top dimension] · Weakest: [bottom dimension] · [View breakdown ▾]

Band labels based on totalScore:
- 0–39: Inadequate
- 40–59: Limited  
- 60–74: Developing
- 75–89: Proficient
- 90–100: Exemplary

When voxScoreProfile is null, show nothing in this section — do not show an empty card or placeholder.

--- 1B. Full breakdown table (collapsed by default) ---

Clicking "View breakdown" toggles open a 7-row table. Each row contains:
- Dimension code and name (e.g. "D1 Subject Knowledge")
- Weight (e.g. "25%")
- Band chip with color coding:
  - Inadequate: red
  - Limited: orange
  - Developing: yellow
  - Proficient: blue
  - Exemplary: green
- Weighted contribution (e.g. "20/25")
- One-line AI rationale from voxScoreProfile.dimensions[n].evidence
- A "View evidence" link — clicking it scrolls to and highlights the transcript in Per-Question Results

Below the table show the score formula:
"VoxScore = sum of (band score × dimension weight × 100). Pass threshold: 60/100."

--- 1C. Auto-open behavior ---

The breakdown table must automatically open (not require a click) when:
- The professor clicks Adjust or Override in the Professor Decision panel
- totalScore is between 55 and 65 (near pass/fail boundary)
- Any dimension has band score 1 (Inadequate) or 2 (Limited)
