QuickVox Phase 4 — VoxPractice Student Self-Training Tool

Goal: Transform QuickVox from a single-question insight tool into a full student self-training coach. Students upload their own study material or pick a subject, the AI generates oral practice questions, quizzes them by voice, and tells them how ready they are. No professor needed. Everything is private to the student.

This is a significant new feature. Build it as a separate flow from the existing QuickVox one-question experience. The existing QuickVox (Phases 1-3) must remain completely untouched.

=== WHAT IS BEING BUILT ===

A student-facing self-training tool called VoxPractice. Entry point is a new "Practice" tab or button on the student dashboard. Four session modes, a voice conversation loop per question, and a readiness report at the end.

=== SCREEN 1 — MATERIAL SETUP ===

Student chooses their practice source. Three options shown as cards:

1. Upload material — student uploads a PDF, DOCX, PPTX, or TXT file. Label: "Most relevant to your coursework — Recommended"
2. Choose a subject — dropdown or searchable list: Engineering, Business, Health Sciences, Education, Law, General Academic Skills
3. Type a topic — free text input with example placeholder: "e.g. Photosynthesis, Supply and Demand, Contract Law"

After source is selected, show a material summary card:
"Found [N] key concepts and [N] possible oral questions across [N] topics."

This builds trust that the AI has actually read the material.

=== SCREEN 2 — SESSION SETUP ===

Student picks session mode — shown as four cards:

1. Warm-up — 3-4 questions, 5-7 minutes. Label: "Low pressure. Build confidence."
2. Readiness Sprint — 7-10 questions, 10-15 minutes. Label: "Main practice mode. Recommended." (default)
3. Weak Spot Review — 4-6 questions, 5-10 minutes. Label: "Focus on what you got wrong before." (disabled on first session, enabled after first session)
4. Mock Oral Exam — 12-18 questions, 20-30 min. Label: "Full simulation. Use when nearly ready."

Student also picks coach style — shown as three options:
- Gentle Coach — supportive, low pressure
- Normal Tutor — balanced (default)
- Strict Examiner — realistic oral defense pressure

Answer length setting: Short (30-45 sec) / Standard (60-90 sec) / Extended (2 min)

=== SCREEN 3 — VOICE SESSION LOOP ===

This is the core experience. One question at a time.

Per-question rhythm:
1. AI displays and speaks the question
2. 15-second prep countdown shown
3. Student records their voice answer (existing recording UI)
4. AI transcribes
5. AI asks ONE follow-up probe based on the answer (e.g. "Can you give an example?" or "Why does that relationship hold?")
6. Student answers the follow-up
7. Brief micro-feedback shown — one strength, one missing point, one retry prompt if needed
8. Move to next question

Micro-feedback format (shown as a small card, not a full rubric):
- "Strong: [one thing done well]"
- "Missing: [one concept or type of evidence not included]"
- Optional: "Try again: [one specific coaching prompt]" — student can retry or skip

The AI must never accidentally answer the question while probing. Follow-up probes are only from the approved list:
- "Can you give an example?"
- "What would happen if the opposite were true?"
- "How does that connect to what you said earlier?"
- "Why does that relationship hold?"
- "What evidence supports your answer?"
- "What assumption are you making here?"

Progress bar shown throughout: "Question 3 of 8"
Label always visible: "Private practice — not sent to your professor"

=== SCREEN 4 — READINESS REPORT ===

Shown after the session ends. Six sections:

1. Readiness indicator — one of four bands:
   - Not ready yet (below 50)
   - Needs targeted practice (50-64)
   - Almost ready (65-79)
   - Ready for oral exam (80-100)
   Note: "This is a practice estimate — not an official grade."

2. VoxScore practice breakdown — same 7-dimension display as the official exam, but labeled "Practice VoxScore — private estimate". Use the existing VoxScore UI components built in Task #33.

3. Concept coverage map — show topics from the uploaded material as colored pills:
   - Green: answered confidently
   - Yellow: partial — mentioned but not fully explained
   - Red: weak — could not explain or missed key points
   - Grey: not tested in this session

4. Best and worst answers — show transcript excerpts:
   - "Your strongest answer: [question] — [excerpt]"
   - "Your weakest answer: [question] — [excerpt + what was missing]"

5. Top 3 fixes — three specific coaching items, not generic:
   - Fix 1: [specific concept or skill to improve]
   - Fix 2: [specific concept or skill to improve]
   - Fix 3: [specific concept or skill to improve]

6. Next session recommendation — one button:
   "Start 7-minute weak spot review" — pre-configured to focus on the weakest concepts from this session

=== AI PROMPT REQUIREMENTS ===

Question generation must use the uploaded material as the only source (RAG against uploaded content). Questions must span cognitive levels:
- Recall: "Define X"
- Understanding: "Explain X in your own words"
- Application: "Given this situation, what would you do?"
- Comparison: "Compare X and Y"
- Reasoning: "Why does X lead to Y?"
- Defense: "I disagree. Defend your answer."

Use gpt-4o for all VoxPractice AI calls — not gpt-4o-mini. This is a coaching tool, quality matters.

Include this clause in every grading prompt: "Score the academic meaning of the answer regardless of whether it is in English, Arabic, or a mix of both. Never penalize language choice, accent, or code-switching unless meaning is materially blocked."

=== DATA AND PRIVACY ===

- All VoxPractice sessions are private to the student — never visible to any professor at any role level
- Session data stored with languageUsed, question, transcript, micro-feedback, and practice VoxScore per question
- No proctoring — no webcam, no screen share, no tab-switch detection
- Label "Private practice — not sent to your professor" always visible during session
- Consent screen required before first voice recording

=== CONSTRAINTS ===

- Do not make VoxPractice scores visible to professors under any circumstances

=== NEW DATABASE TABLE ===

Create a new practice_sessions table:
- id, studentId, createdAt, completedAt
- sourceType: 'upload' | 'subject' | 'topic'
- sourceSummary: text (concept count, topic count)
- sessionMode: 'warmup' | 'readiness_sprint' | 'weak_spot' | 'mock_oral'
- coachStyle: 'gentle' | 'normal' | 'strict'
- questions: jsonb array (question text, transcript, micro-feedback, voxScoreProfile per question)
- overallReadinessScore: real (0-100)
- overallVoxScoreProfile: jsonb (VoxScoreProfile)
- conceptCoverageMap: jsonb (topic -> status)
- languageUsed: text
- completedQuestionCount: integer
