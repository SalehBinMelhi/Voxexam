I want to evolve VoxExams from an AI oral exam grading system into a concept-level learning intelligence system.

We are not adding random features. We are redesigning the product so it tracks true understanding and reduces shallow learning and cheating.

Implement the following in structured phases.

PHASE 1 — Concept Tagging System

Every question must support 1–3 “concept tags”.

Concepts represent learning outcomes or core knowledge areas.

Examples: “force”, “acceleration”, “thermodynamics”, “critical thinking”, etc.

These must be stored structurally in the database (not free text).

When a professor creates or edits a question:

They must assign concept tags to that question.

The UI must allow selecting or creating concept tags per course.

When a student submission is graded:

The AI scores (correctness + understanding) must also be mapped to each concept attached to that question.

This creates per-concept performance data.

Do not duplicate raw data. Use derived aggregation from existing submission and score structures.

PHASE 2 — Student Concept Performance Engine

Create a system that computes, per student:

Average correctness across all submissions.

Average understanding across all submissions.

Performance aggregated per concept.

Top 3 strongest concepts.

Top 3 weakest concepts.

Concept-level improvement trend over time.

Overall improvement trend (first 3 vs last 3 submissions).

This must be computed on demand via optimized queries.
Do not create new persistent tables unless necessary.
Avoid materialized views at this stage.

The system must support longitudinal tracking (performance across time).

PHASE 3 — Student Dashboard (Understanding-Based, Not Grade-Based)

Design a student-facing dashboard that shows:

Overall Understanding Level (not just exam score).

Concept Strength Map:

Strong areas

Weak areas

Improvement Direction:

Improving

Declining

Stable

Suggested Focus Areas:

Automatically generated based on weakest concepts.

Simple Integrity Indicator:

Neutral phrasing (not accusatory)

Based on suspicious behavior patterns.

The tone must be developmental, not punitive.
The goal is to guide learning, not punish.

PHASE 4 — Follow-Up Oral Micro-Check Trigger

Add logic that automatically flags students for follow-up oral verification when:

Understanding is low but correctness is high.

Suspicious behavior frequency exceeds a threshold.

Improvement trend is sharply negative.

When triggered:

The system should allow the professor to assign a short follow-up oral micro-check (2–3 new concept-aligned questions).

This reinforces authentic understanding and reduces cheating incentives.

Do not implement live video infrastructure changes yet.
Focus on logic and trigger mechanism.

PHASE 5 — Professor Concept Insight View

Create a professor-facing view that shows:

Class-wide concept mastery heatmap.

Most misunderstood concepts.

Distribution of understanding vs correctness.

Students flagged for follow-up.

Improvement trends per student.

The goal is to shift the professor view from “who passed?” to “who actually understands?”

SYSTEM PRINCIPLES

Do not refactor core grading pipeline.

Do not change authentication or RBAC.

Do not introduce unnecessary architectural complexity.

Build this as a layered intelligence system on top of existing submission data.

Keep everything explainable and auditable.

Prioritize clarity over visual complexity.

The objective is to transform VoxExams into a structured oral learning intelligence platform that exposes real understanding, reduces shallow memorization, and discourages cheating by design.

Start with Phase 1 and Phase 2.
